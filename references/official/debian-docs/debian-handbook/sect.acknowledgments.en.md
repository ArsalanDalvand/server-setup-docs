				In 2013, the release of Debian 7 gave us a good opportunity to discuss a new contract with Eyrolles. We convinced them that a license more in line with the Debian values would contribute to the book's success. That wasn't an easy deal to make, and we agreed to set up another [crowdfunding campaign](https://www.ulule.com/liberation-cahier-admin-debian/) to cover some of the costs and reduce the risks involved.
